# Biopolymer films for sustainable packaging

LaTeX source and figures for **Towards Sustainable Packaging: Improving Properties of Biopolymer Films Using Fruit Purées**.

**Award:** First prize at the Institute of Physics Sustainable Materials Symposium.

**Authors:** Douglas Penning, Alvin Zhu, Gabriel Yang and Manjula Silva.  
**Affiliation:** Department of Materials, Imperial College London.

## Research question

How do papaya and cantaloupe purées change the optical, wetting and mechanical properties of starch–gelatine films, and how do these effects depend on processing temperature?

The poster presents measurements of visible and ultraviolet transmittance, water contact angle and tensile behaviour. It discusses the trade-off between optical improvement and mechanical performance when selecting a composition for a packaging application.

## Interpretation

The reported results belong to the formulations and processing conditions studied. The poster shows repeat variability with standard-deviation error bars; its findings should not be generalised to all biopolymer packaging.

This repository is a presentation archive. It contains the poster source and prepared figures, rather than a complete raw-data or analysis-code release. Consult the author list, references and image credits in the source when reusing or citing the work.
